Privacy Policy

KidStory (kidstory.online website and the KidStory mobile apps) · Last updated: June 9, 2026

KidStory makes interactive storybooks for young children. We designed it to collect as little data as possible — and from children, none at all.

What the mobile apps collect

Nothing. The KidStory apps have no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking SDKs. Stories run entirely on the device. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information from anyone, including children.

In-app purchases

Optional premium story content can be bought as a one-time in-app purchase. Payment is processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their own privacy policies. We never see your name, payment details, or any account information. Purchase entitlement is remembered by your Apple/Google account and cached on your device so it stays unlocked forever, including after reinstalling ("Restore Purchases").

What the website collects

The kidstory.online website uses Google Analytics with IP anonymization to count visits (no advertising features, no personalized signals). If you voluntarily submit our contact form (a parent/guardian feature), we receive the name, email address, and message you typed, and we use them only to reply to you. We never sell or share this information.

Children's privacy (COPPA)

Our content is made for children, but our apps collect no data from any user, so no personal information from children is ever collected, used, or disclosed. The website's contact form is intended for parents and guardians only.

Data retention & your rights

Since the apps collect nothing, there is nothing to retain or delete. For contact-form emails, write to us and we will delete your correspondence on request.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
